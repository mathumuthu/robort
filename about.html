A team obsessed with intelligent machines

Founded in 2014, NexBot began as a small robotics lab with one belief: machines should adapt to people, not the other way around. Today we deploy autonomous systems across manufacturing, healthcare and logistics.

Our multidisciplinary team blends deep expertise in machine learning, computer vision, mechanical engineering and human-centered design.
