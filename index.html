FAQ

NexBot AI & robotics FAQs

AI and robotics have applications in manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, transportation, customer service, finance and entertainment. They automate tasks, enhance productivity, improve accuracy and drive innovation across industries.

AI is the development of algorithms, models and systems that enable machines to mimic human intelligence and behavior — including learning from data, recognizing patterns, making decisions and improving over time.

Robotics is the engineering discipline focused on designing, building and operating robots — machines that can sense their environment, process information and perform physical tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously.

AI is the "brain" — software that learns and makes decisions. Robotics is the "body" — the physical machine that acts. When combined, you get intelligent robots that can perceive, reason and interact with the real world.
